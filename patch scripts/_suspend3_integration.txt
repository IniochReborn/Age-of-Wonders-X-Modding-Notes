SUSPEND (214) RANGE FIX - 2026-10-16 (fork; NOT applied live)
Root cause: tactical range = byte +5 of the spell's combat info (VMT+0x7C GetCombatInfo).  Combat spells copy
[spell+0x34..0x3B] into it (TCombatSpell.GetCombatInfo 0x557F7258: +4 shots, +5 RANGE, +6 fast-combat targets, +7 side),
but TUnitSpell.GetCombatInfo 0x5577AC78 (TRemedy and every other unit spell) hard-codes +5 = 0xFF (= unlimited) at
0x5577AC91, after the side byte.  TRemedy's [spell+0x38] = 12 is never read by the tactical cursor, so no pipeline poke
can change it (Static Shield's (0x38,4,-1) poke is likewise a no-op; it is unlimited anyway via the 0xFF).
Fix = patch_suspend_fix_v1.py v2 (upgraded in place): the GCI cave (same address 0x5586D820, still 31 B < its 32-B slot,
RL cave unmoved) for spell 214 writes side 3 + range 0x0C (Great Hail / Slow / Remedy's 12) and jumps to the epilogue
0x5577AC95, skipping the 0xFF write; every other unit spell is untouched.  No pipeline change.
