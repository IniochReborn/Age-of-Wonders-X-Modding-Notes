patch_overflow_army_v1.py - post-combat overflow placement (2026-10-18), NOT applied to the live DLL.

Problem: units that join you in combat (Dominate/Charm/side change, also combat-summoned units without a strategic
party) are placed in TCombatUnit.Finalize pass 2 via TAbstractUnit.Place(flags 1 = spiral) starting AT THE COMBAT
TARGET hex -> a new army there.  ArmyCombatMoveTE.ExecuteCombat then PlacePrivate's the moving party (units with
[unit+0x25]&4) onto that same hex without a merge check -> two armies on one hex when the party is full.

Fix: pass-2 call sites 0x55724EA9 / 0x55724ED1 / 0x55724F0E -> cave 0x55882820 (zone 0x55882800-0x55882C00).
If (moving units on the attacker hex + own units already on the target + 1) > TArmy.MaxSize and the owners match,
the spiral starts at radius 1 (same Place/CanPlace rules as summons, one hex per call); nothing fits -> vanilla.
Otherwise vanilla Place, untouched.
