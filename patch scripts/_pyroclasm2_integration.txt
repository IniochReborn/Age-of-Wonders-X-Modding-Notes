== 4. Rules / rates (constants at the top of patch_pyroclasm_v1.py) ==
  Lava under units: allowed.  Every terrain change of ours passes an unplaced TFrozenWaterHS as ChangeTerrain's
    SOURCE -> TArmyHS.CanChangeTerrain no longer vetoes it (ClassID 0x2016C exemption) and TArmyHS.TerrainChanged ->
    TArmy.ValidateTerrain(Ex) kills every unit that cannot stand on lava with the vanilla event (the same path as a
    Fire-Halo unit losing the halo on lava / an army drowning when frozen water melts).  Fire-immune units survive.
  Snow melt: surface SNOW (bare / vegetation) next to OUR lava -> steppe, at creation and every caster turn.  PERMANENT
    (not reverted).  Snow forest, hills, roads, structures and other spells' hexes are not melted.
  Bursts: first cast -> every map volcano 1..CAST_MAX (3) lava once (marker 0xE on its anchor; re-armed if the spell
    ends and is cast again); a fresh conversion -> 1..CONV_MAX (2).  Bursts are outside the per-turn budget.
  Spread: never-spawned lava 1 in 2, spawned lava 1 in SPAWNED_DIV (10) per turn (marker +0xC bit 1).  Volcano
    eruptions stay 1 in 2.  Budgets: surface area/300 + 2 new lava per turn (44x34 -> 6, 96x96 -> 32), each
    underground level area/400 + 1 (44x34 -> 4, 96x96 -> 24).
  Underground / depths: every lava hex (map or ours) 1 in 2 (10% once it spawned; map lava gets a code-9 marker the
    first time) -> one random direction onto DIRT only (earth / rock / cave water / cave ice never); armies allowed.
  Revert (no player has an active Pyroclasm; each player's own markers on his turn): countdown 2..5 turns per hex,
    then surface lava -> WASTELAND, underground lava -> its original terrain (dirt), our volcano -> the wasteland
    MOUNTAIN of the same size (same footprint, same anchor).  Map-volcano (0xE) and map-lava (9) markers are just
    dropped.  A re-cast during the revert clears all countdowns (spreading resumes).
