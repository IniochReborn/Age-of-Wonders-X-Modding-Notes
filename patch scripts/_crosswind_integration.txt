DESIGN / ENGINE (patch_crosswind_v1.py, AoWEPACK only):
  * 213 = THighPrayer clone (instant, infinite range).  TC makes one TTacticalCombatHighPrayerCA per FRIENDLY unit; our
    `hx` (chained in front of Life/Air's hx on the CA Execute slot 0x557F4520) runs instead of the High Prayer body
    (no heal / no 0x64 blessing): parent exec 0x557F6C84, then walks the combat object list ([[ctx+0xC]+0x34]+8) and
    ExpandAbility(0xC2) on every living (([+0x47]&9)==0) TCombatUnit of ANOTHER combat player ([+0x45]) than the
    caster whose unit lacks 0xC2 - idempotent, so the N friendly CAs give one effect.  Machines included.
  * 0xC2 = a THIRD TBurningAbility instance (Rot's recipe): mode 2 combat rounds, duration 3, combat-only, own round
    countdown/removal; `tk` (chained in front of Rot's def cave at 0x557BA459) skips Burning's 1-damage roll for 0xC2.
  * -2 ranged ATK: `ra` on both GetAttackRA epilogues (0x5576E68E / 0x5576E6A5): unit holds 0xC2 (VMT+0x88, guarded
    [esi+4]) -> bl -= 2 (floor 0).  Melee (TUnit.GetAttack) and spells untouched; the unit panel's ranged value shows it.
  * fh / ai: THighPrayer VMT+0xD8 / +0xD0 chained (in front of Life/Air's): 213 is never auto-cast in fast combat and
    never cast by the tactical AI (same as Heavenly Chorus).
