WHAT THE PUFF IS
  The pushed unit's hop carrier (kind 0x2F, tagged Gust 212) runs WoF's per-tick swirl loop (0x42BFA5..0x42C0E8). Every
  tick it spawns one white PARTICLE.ILB orb for EVERY path index in [tick-20, tick]: about 21 per tick, about 315 per push.
