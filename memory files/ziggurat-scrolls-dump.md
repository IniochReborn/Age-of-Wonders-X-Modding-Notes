## ⚠ FOLLOW-UPS FLAGGED FOR OUR MOD (verify before/at next release cycle)
1. **Custom spells 0xC8–0xCB (200–203) may sit past a hard 131-byte wall.** His `ID_Ceilings.md`: AoWTCPCK `AoWTC.SpellTypes @0x004672F4` is exactly 131 bytes; 13 hardware `BOUND [0..130]` reads → ERangeError if a tactical path sees id≥131; `SpellTypes[200]` actually reads `BreathDir` bytes (nonzero garbage — possibly why our book check passes!), and WRITING SpellTypes[id>130] corrupts `BreathHit @0x00467378`/`BreathDir @0x004673A8`. He explicitly judged our "spell working at id 202" as "a latent range-check error, not a working spell". Our spells are user-confirmed working — so verify WHERE our summon-mermaid "SpellTypes[id] byte" fix actually wrote, and whether any BOUND path can see our ids. Honest fix if real = relocate the array (12 disp32 refs + DATA ptr 0x004693F8 + 9 bound pairs + export RVA + AoW.exe IAT slot 0x0045EBEC).
2. **.pfs writes must recompute the trailing CRC-32** (region `data[4:-4]`; validity check `zlib.crc32(d[4:])&0xFFFFFFFF == 0x2144DF1C` — that constant is the CRC residue, NOT a magic). 9 files carry it (Unitres, Ability, Spells, TEXT, HERORES, FACERES, ITEMGFX, Unitgfx, comb_res); General/HEROES/ITEMS/RulesV have no wrapper — never append one. He flags that "the shared AoWx notes ignore the tail" ⇒ audit whether our pfs-editing scripts fixed the CRC (the game may not verify — our edits work — but AoWEd does write it).
3. **Bug he found in our `build_mod_manual.py`:** hero-availability reads `(mask&0x80)and(mask&0x100)`; the engine tests `byte[ability+0x21]&1` — use `0x100|0x200`. Coincidentally right for all 96 vanilla abilities, mislabels any new one.
4. **Our raze-framework share doc has a wrong VMT table**: TStructure's VMT ends at +0x1EC (CanRaze last); the +0x1F0/+0x1FC/+0x200 rows were a union of TExplorationSite/TTower branches. Cross-check [[reflecting-pool-raze]] claims (our +0x14C/+0x150/+0x158 core slots are fine).
5. **Two of our believed constraints are REFUTED:** (a) "ability ids ≤171 / >0xA9 crashes Localize" — false (his Drillmaster 0xAB + Magebane 0xAA confirmed in-game); real rule: **above id 169, passive classes only** (AoWTCPCK `BOUND [0..169]` on `AbilTypes @0x00467248` — only selectable abilities' `[tcu+0x34]` reaches it; TEnhancement/TStrike GetControlType=0 are safe; Touch/Ranged/Healing/Dispel/SpellCasting are NOT) unless the 9 bound pairs are raised; plus in-combat panel/banners/AI ceilings `cmp 0xAA` sites (AoWTCPCK 0x42354A + 4×EvalBattle; exe 0x406DEF/0x455DE1). (b) The 0xCD stateful-id save ceiling (tag 0x32+id byte) was ALSO refuted later — wide property entries round-trip (keys to 0x11D). Update [[ability-id-map]] thinking accordingly. Correct error pairing: **217 = duplicate ability id, 216 = heap corruption** (e.g. shortstring length-byte over-run), sparseness harmless.
