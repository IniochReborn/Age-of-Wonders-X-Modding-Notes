Casts via the human unit-spell TE with caster discount + budget manager record. Tunables KEEP_PCT/RES_PCT/MAXCASTS/REQUIRE_UPKEEP/UPKEEP_GUARD. Recruit ZONE_END narrowed to 0x5581D400.
